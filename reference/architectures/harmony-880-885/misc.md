# Harmony 880 and 885: miscellaneous

**The rule for this file** is the model folders' rule: a note goes here only when it fits none of the
other files, and a kind of note that recurs gets a file of its own.

## Why a full folder for an architecture nobody here has measured

Every fact on these pages comes from published configurations and firmware images. The folder has the
same files as one whose remote has been measured, so that a measurement on a unit replaces a footnote in
place and moves nothing.

## Names

* **Espresso** is the platform's codename, in the comments of Logitech's own firmware packages and in its
  classic client.[^s7][^s207]
* Logitech's classic client names skin 39 **Espresso Pro** and skins 44 and 45 **Corona**, for two
  regions; its live service calls the same three the Harmony 880 Pro, the Harmony 720 and the Harmony
  785.[^s131]
* **The 880 and the 885 are one remote under two regional names**, as the 720 and the 785 are: a
  community comparison table records the 880 as `Eur#885` and the 720 as `Eur#785`.[^x-forum-table]
  Which skin carries the European name, and what else differs between the two, is in the
  [Harmony 885's folder](../../remotes/harmony-885/README.md).
* **Skin 46 is in two of the client's groups**, Espresso and Mocha, alone among all 52 skins it places,
  and nothing explains it; both groups reach the same driver, so the client cannot notice.[^s207]
