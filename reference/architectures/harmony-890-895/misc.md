# Harmony 890 and 895: miscellaneous

**The rule for this file** is the model folders' rule: a note goes here only when it fits none of the
other files, and a kind of note that recurs gets a file of its own.

## Why a full folder for an architecture known only from configurations

Every fact on these pages comes from configurations their owners published, and no firmware image exists.
The folder has the same files as one whose remote has been measured, so that a measurement on a unit or a
firmware image replaces a footnote in place and moves nothing.

## Names

* **Cappuccino** is the platform's codename, in the comments of Logitech's own firmware packages and in its
  classic client.[^s7][^s207]
* Logitech's classic client names skin 40 **Cappuccino Pro**; its live service calls it the Harmony 890
  Pro.[^s131]
* **The 890 and the 895 are one remote under two regional names**: a community comparison table records the
  890 as `Eur#895`.[^x-forum-table]
* **Skins 20 and 24 are not remotes.** Logitech's classic client lists them as a Harmony 891 and 896 on the
  Whisky platform, beside Cappuccino on the same transport, and its live service calls both an RF Wireless
  Extender.[^s207][^s131]
