# What Logitech's compiler writes for the Harmony One

### The firmware's own wiring on the Harmony One

Arch 12 (Harmony One) only; [findings.md](../../../docs/findings.md) section 410. Built by `buildTouchWiring` in
`packages/codec/src/families/touch/wiring.ts` from a description `describeTouchWiring` reads, byte for byte on the
19 compiles of section 405 and on seven configurations of ours, with every generated byte overwritten in the input
first. The engine, an instruction's, a list's and an entry's encoding and the swap into a layout, is
`wiringparts.ts`, shared with the arch 14 wiring above; `Family.wiring.check` is how shared code asks for either.

| structure | generated | the description's |
|---|---|---|
| base slot 2, base slot 15 | whole, section 407 | nothing |
| base slot 4 | code `k` to mode `10 + k`, `k` 0 to 29, fallback 10, the ten firmware screens | nothing |
| base slot 8's leading list | `call start` | the start list's index |
| base slot 12 | every record's kind | the rest of each record: the table's order is read off a record's duration and opcode, the screen light pair's durations are settings, and a device's timer is carried whole |
| base slot 9 entries 0 to 6 and the leftover entry | tags, flags, form, stored order, every opcode | what the bindings name; the leftover entry's index |
| base slot 10's front | 168 lists, entries 1 to 4's own in stored order, indices from the first | where the front starts, three past the start list on 19 of 19 |
| the other lists the wiring reaches | every instruction | their indices and the modes, variables, value maps and screen programs they name |
| the 14 lists reached only through the transitions of `readyVar`, `relightVar` and `settleVar`, section 418 | every instruction | their indices: the four the transitions call off the variables' base slot 13 records, the ten below off their parents' calls |
| settings | | the Remote Assistant at start, the ladder's `07 FFFB`, the screen light's two durations |

**The settings**: the Remote Assistant on 18 of 19, the leftover entry wide with it and narrow without, and
without it the ladder is its last rung; `07 FFFB`, section 73's cancel of all four running timers, opening the
ladder on 2 of the 18, what decides it *not established*, and refused without the assistant; the screen light pair
10 and 20 seconds on all 19.

**Base slot 9's fixed entries**: entry 0 binds events `0x1A` to `0x1D` to the events list; entry 1 binds
fourteen tags, ten events and the presses of scans 34, 37, 46 and 47, `0x23`, `0xAF`, `0x26`, `0xAE`, `0x27`, `0x25` (`00 0000`), `0xA2`, `0xA5` (select the leftover
entry), `0x0F`, `0x2D` (`07 FFFA`), `0x10`, `0x19` (`07 FFF6`), `0x1F` and `0x1E`; entries 2 and 3 bind the
presses of scans 1 to 40, 43 to 53 and 55 and tag `0x30` each to a list of its own, entry 2's
`[07 FFFB, call ready, 3F C11C]` and entry 3's `[07 FFFB, call menu, call lights]`, entry 3 adding `0x17` and
`0x26` to the menu list and `0x1F` to `07 FFFB`; entry 4 binds the presses to `[call lights, call keyLights]`
each, `0x30` to the lights and `0x17` to a list of its own; entry 5 binds `0x1E` to `07 FFFB`; entry 6 binds
`0x18` and `0x19` to the events list. The leftover entry binds `0x01`, `0x05` and the presses of scans 34, 38 and 55, `0xA2` (the
assistant's variable to 1, with flag 1, absent without the assistant), `0xA6` (a value map) and `0xB7`. Entries 0, 3, 4, 5 and 6 are
wide, entries 1 and 2 narrow. Stored in `tagSlotOrder` with the ties `0xAF` before `0x26`, `0xAE` before
`0x27`, `0x0F` before `0x2D` in entry 1; `0x17` before `0x9E`, `0x1F` before `0x96`, `0x26` before `0xAF` in
entry 3; `0x17` before `0x9E` in entry 4; `0xA6` before `0xB7` in the leftover entry.

**The timers**, every one kind 1:

```
screen light, its partner   the setting's seconds, 10 and 20   call a list switching the lights off
tick                        2 s     call
lights out                  1 s     call
two of 5 s                  5 s     call, one of them started by entry 1's 0x26 list
enter                       5 s     7E a screen of the configuration's
six of 1 s                  1 s     7E a screen each, the fifth the ready list's
three second                3 s     call [07 FFFB, call lights, enter a screen], on 2 of 19, started by the screen the power off value map enters (the attempt screen on `harvest_one_families_1`)
redraw k                    4 s     call [73 clock, 73 charging gauge, 1F EB00|k], 13 to 20 per compile, one per class of screen
a device's                  carried whole, on 12 of 19
```

The thirteen are on 19 of 19 once each, in **three orders** over the 19, five with the three second timer's
place, *what orders them not established*. A record is generated in its kind byte alone: the reader names each
timer by its duration and opcode, and the two pairs those cannot tell apart by the list starting each, so a
screen light of 1 to 5 seconds would share a length with another timer and is not read.

*Not established*: what `07 FFFA`, the `3F C1xx`, `3F F0xx` and `1F FExx` steps do on this remote; what the five
one second timers' screens other than the ready list's are for; what decides the ladder's `07 FFFB`.

**The redraw timers are one per class of screen**, section 430. Every redraw timer is started by the enter
handler (tag 6) of at least one mode, bound directly or in the list it calls, and no mode starts two: 3195
modes over the 19 start one, none starts two and no redraw timer is started by none. P and Q are the clock header's
clock and then its charging gauge on 19 of 19. The screens starting one timer, named by the screen sets' finders on
the sixteen English compiles of skin 54:

|---|---|

| activity menu | the activity menu |

| device lists | every device list |
| sensitivity confirmations | the five, four of the Remote Assistant's screens, and four screens per activity |

| Screen Sensitivity | Screen Sensitivity |

So a configuration with no activity holds thirteen, 4 of 4, and the activities' start up, working and other screens
add classes of their own, 0 to 7 over the sixteen. The welcome tour's eleven screens start none. The classes' order
in the table is one compile's, `harvest_one_two_devices`', and not a rule: the sixteen hold 14 orders. *Not
established*: what makes a class, which no byte of the screens states, and how the working screens are grouped (one
to four classes, a review's measurement and not tested).

#### The Harmony One's pads, device pages, assistant and key lights

**A pad's label, the Harmony One**, section 394: a box 81 by 50 at one of six places, its name in the
configuration's pad font (base slot 7 index 5, 7, 8 or 9, read off the device pages) split into words at

eighteen compiles whose device modes are read; in the 14 pixel set those labels use it puts a one line label's
capitals 2 pixels below the pad's middle, and a
configuration of ours moves each label's y to centre its capitals, `withCentredPadLabels`, section 399.

**A device page's closing and its two keys below the screen, the Harmony One**, section 401: every device page
program ends on a narrow switch of two arms over the activity running variable (its number per compile), arm 0
drawing "Activities" and arm 1 "Current Activity" (in the configuration's language), both jumping to the byte after the switch, which draws
"Devices" and the page counter and ends; 571 of 571 pages over the eighteen compiles with device pages. A page
whose hit page offers scans 43 and 44 binds both in its own tagged list, before its rows: 43 to `0x72` on the
working screen record, 44 to a list `[75 0FCA, 72 <device list record>]`; 508 of 508. A composer copies both
from the configuration's own device page, `deviceBottomKeys`.

**An activity's device list and its start up title, the Harmony One**, section 402: each activity has a device
list of its own holding every device once, 64 of 64 over the fifteen compiles with an activity, as some devices
(the activity's own and on 32 another) and then the rest in the idle list's order; the head is the devices the
activity's start switches on, in that order, on 61 and refused on 3, section 424. A compile with an activity holds two font sets
of face One15 and draws every start up title in one of them, at y 124, found by that, `startupTitleSet`.
Screen instruction 1, operands `0 0 176 220 0 0`, sits one call deep on every page of the working screen on 23
of 64 activities and is unread.

**The Remote Assistant at start, the Harmony One**, section 403: one unnamed state variable, running 0 to 3, tested
by a ladder of lists loading 1, 2 and 3 into the byte register (`1F FB0k`, then a test `71`), the first two rungs
entering the welcome and the "devices off" screens (English on 17, Dutch on the friend's), the third going on with
the start; 18 of 19 compiles, none on `one_config`. Yes on the hide question writes 3, and on 14 of the 18 the
activities' assistant screens write 2 and 3 too. It starts at 0 in Logitech's compiles; starting it at 3 starts the
remote on its ordinary screen, `withoutStartupAssistant`. The "Welcome!" screen follows the "devices off" screen's
OK; its own variable, 0 to 1, is written 0 silently by a list of every compile, so its starting value is not what
decides it.

**The welcome tour and the start's Remote Assistant left out, the Harmony One**, section 421, `withoutTourAndAssistant`
in `families/touch/withouttour.ts`, the composed path's default: the sixteen modes of section 412 go with base slot 6's

**The keys' lights, the Harmony One**, section 400: a three instruction list `[3F C0a2, 3F C0b2, write 1]`
sets channels `a` and `b` of the device at `0x60` on, the same with `C0a0`, `C0b0` and a write of 0 off.

the 408 others. `withPageKeyLights`. The screen light is the 10 second timer of the one pair of timers queueing
two lists of the same content, 10 and 20 seconds, in all nineteen compiles; its index varies, so it is found by
that shape.
### The screen sections by category, the Harmony One

The same cut, section 405, with the touch map, base slot 17, as one unit per hit page. **The head of the mode
table** is the event map's fallback, the number of firmware screens, plus its 30 status codes: 40 modes on the
One, 3 binding a keypad and 37 not, and 44 or 49 on arch 14, where this agrees with section 317's reading. A configuration
whose events all name one mode carries no status screens, as ours, and there the head is section 317's run. A
page's `0x16` call into its chrome is a transfer in the normal form; each chrome is called by one mode.

the screens structure calls `other`. That rules out a screen naming a device or an activity and not one a
