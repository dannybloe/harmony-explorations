# Harmony 300: behaviour

What the remote does in use. **Nothing has been seen at the bench about the 300 in use**: no infrared bench
run exists for it, and what follows is Logitech's setup guide and what its configurations say.

## Device mode, without a screen

"To operate a device just click the device you want to operate and you're in control. To turn a device on
or off, click the device you want to operate and then press the power button." Logitech's setup guide,
"Using Your Harmony 300". So a device key is device mode, and each of the four holds one device, the
group of the same index in the configuration, section 265.

## Watch TV

"Turn your TV and cable/ satellite box on with a single button press", Logitech's setup guide. What the
configuration does on that key is [features.md](features.md)'s open question.

## What the configurations say

Every send is paired with a `0x7C`, on the five configurations of this architecture, read from
configurations, section 278. A second hand unit arrived with a configuration built in 2011 by its own
timestamp, and was read as it arrived and again after Logitech's service programmed four devices on it,
measured, sections 264 and 265.
