# Harmony 300: hardware

What is inside, as far as anything here can say, which is little: no 300 has been opened. The processor,
its internal flash, the batteries and the moulding are the family's, stated once in
[the family's README](../../architectures/harmony-300-350/README.md) and
[memory.md](../../architectures/harmony-300-350/memory.md).

The one thing that is the 300's own is the printing on its keys, [keys.md](keys.md). The case and every
key are the Harmony 350's part[^t-moulding].

## Not checked

* The processor and storage parts, the RAM size.
* Infrared transmitters, range, learning, backlight, size and weight: the setup guide states none.
* Whether the 300's device keys light while sending, as the 350's manual says the 350's do.

[^t-moulding]: Measured on a unit. [The test](../../../packages/silhouettes/test/models.test.ts)
