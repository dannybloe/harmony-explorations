# Harmony 785: behaviour

How a Harmony 785 behaves for the person holding it. What activities and device mode are, on every Harmony, is
[how a Harmony works](../../how-a-harmony-works.md).

**Nothing about this model's own behaviour has been measured here.** Its application is the Harmony 720's build,
whose start check is the platform's with its working addresses moved,
[the platform's firmware](../../architectures/harmony-880-885/firmware.md#the-harmony-720s-images).[^t-785-first-read][^t-885-start]

## Not checked

* How device mode is entered and left, the screens on start, on the cable and with a low battery, the help and
  the timers.[^unchecked]
