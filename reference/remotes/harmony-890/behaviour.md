# Harmony 890: behaviour

How a Harmony 890 behaves for the person holding it. What activities and device mode are, on every
Harmony, is [how a Harmony works](../../how-a-harmony-works.md).

**Nothing about this model's own behaviour is known here.** No unit has been held and no manual of it has
been read, and a configuration does not say how the remote enters device mode or what it shows on
start.[^unchecked]

## Not checked

* How device mode is entered and left, the screens on start, on the cable and with low batteries, the
  help, the timers, and how the radio channels behave.[^unchecked]

[^unchecked]: Not checked.
