# Harmony 890: hardware

What is inside a Harmony 890 or 895 is known only as far as its configurations and the comparison table
say. The platform is described once in [the architecture folder](../../architectures/harmony-890-895/README.md).

| | value |
|---|---|
| keypad | the Harmony 885's 55 populated positions on its 4 by 16 lattice, on a Harmony 895, [keys.md](keys.md)[^s177] |
| radio | six channels[^x-forum-table] |

## Not checked

* The processor, the flash, the batteries, the transmitters, the radio and the learning sensor.[^unchecked]

[^s177]: Measured in configurations. [Section 177](../../../docs/findings.md#s177)
[^x-forum-table]: Stated by a third party. [The comparison table](https://www.harmony-remote-forum.de/harmony_compare.php)
[^unchecked]: Not checked.
