# Todo

**The sequence lives here and nowhere else.** This file holds the FHSDK's work: the libraries, the reference and the
tools an application such as FreeHarmony builds on. Nothing of FreeHarmony itself is in it; that is
[todo/todo-fh.md](todo/todo-fh.md), kept for reference until a new FreeHarmony todo is written, once the FHSDK is far
enough. What the FHSDK must be able to do is [the requirements](docs/remote-requirements.md), R1 and on, and how far
each bench remote has it is its checklist, chapter 1. Chapters 2 to 11 are the abilities with open work, in the order
an application needs them, each item naming the requirements it serves; chapters 12 and 13 hold what stands apart.

Either of us adds, ticks or removes items. Keep the descriptions to one line: the reasoning belongs in
a plan, in `docs/decisions.md`, or in `docs/findings.md` with a test. A worked out plan goes in `docs/plans/`
and is linked from its item. An ability whose steps do not fit a few lines has a file of its own in `todo/`.

**Every item asks the architecture question before it is ticked**, decision 16: does this hold on the
other architectures, and if it was not checked, the item says so. "Not checked" and "no image exists"
are complete answers; an unasked question is not. Section 274 is what prompted it, where a firmware
reading stated as a fact about Harmonys turned out to be arch 12's and the Harmony 525 does it
differently.

*Replaced `docs/plans/002-the-roadmap.md` on 6 September 2026, which carried three separate accounts of the same
sequence. The reasoning it held is `docs/plans/002-the-roadmap.md`, its decisions are
`docs/decisions.md`. Restructured around the FHSDK by [plan 015](docs/plans/015-requirements-and-capabilities.md);
finished tracks are in `todo/done/`.*
